\markboth{Reading list}{Reading list}

This table gathers the reading named at the end of each chapter so that the
whole plan can be seen at a glance. Each row lists the \emph{core} reading for
that session, followed by \emph{further} or optional deepening; the
per-chapter blocks give the same references with more context. Page numbers
refer to the editions below; for the CSA Security Guidance they are the
printed page numbers in the document's footer. The weekly Microsoft Learn
module is a graded companion, not a substitute for the reading.

\paragraph{Core texts.}
\emph{CSA} --- Cloud Security Alliance (2024), \emph{Security Guidance for
Critical Areas of Focus in Cloud Computing v5}.
\emph{Comer} --- D.~E.~Comer (2021), \emph{The Cloud Computing Book: The Future
of Computing Explained}, Chapman \& Hall/CRC.
\emph{J\o sang} --- A.~J\o sang (2024), \emph{Cybersecurity: Technology and
Governance}, Springer, ISBN 978-3-031-68483-8.
\emph{Tanenbaum and Bos} --- A.~S.~Tanenbaum and H.~Bos (2015), \emph{Modern
Operating Systems}, 4th edition, Pearson; drawn on for operating-systems
foundations in Sessions~2 and~7.
